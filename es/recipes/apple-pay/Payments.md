# Accept or modify a payment

Commerce Hub simplifies payment acceptance integration and provides access to a robust suite of global commerce solutions. It supports a variety of payment methods, including [debit cards and credit cards](?path=docs/Resources/Guides/Payment-Sources/Payment-Card.md), [Pay by Bank](?path=docs/Resources/Guides/Payment-Sources/Tokenization/Pay-by-Bank-Token.md), [Digital Wallets](?path=docs/Getting-Started/Getting-Started-Wallets.md), and [tokenization](?path=docs/Resources/API-Documents/Payments_VAS/Payment-Token.md).
